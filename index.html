Changelog

v0.13 · 26 November 2026

  • Faint far clouds on the horizon, drifting and changing slowly.

v0.12 · 26 November 2026

  • Opens about a third sooner.
  • A little lighter on the graphics chip again, with the same picture.

v0.11 · 25 November 2026

  • Smoother and cooler: about a quarter less work for the graphics chip, with the same picture.
  • Visits are counted now: a number per day, nothing about you.

v0.10 · 3 November 2026

  • Phones draw at 1.5× by default: cooler, still sharp. Settings → Quality → Resolution changes it.
  • The 3D engine now loads from this site, not a third-party server.

v0.9 · 3 November 2026

  • Runs cooler on phones and fast screens, with the same picture.

v0.8 · 28 October 2026

  • Raindrops on the glass when it rains: a few slow drops in light rain, many running streams in a storm, each drop showing the scene upside down, by day and by night. They drift with the wind, and fewer reach the glass when the wind blows from behind you. Settings → Quality → Rain on the glass turns them off.
  • Fireflies are small points of light now, closer to real life.

v0.7 · 28 October 2026

  • Real, soft three-dimensional clouds: sunlight shines through their thin edges, lights their tops and leaves their undersides in shade.
  • Cloud shadows on the land take the shape of the clouds, and sunbeams slant down through the gaps between them.
  • An overcast sky is a textured grey-white blanket above the mountain, and a crown of cloud swirls round the summit.

v0.6 · 28 October 2026

  • Distant rain falls in grey curtains over the valley on rainy and stormy days.

v0.5 · 28 October 2026

  • More butterflies and fireflies, and easier to see; fireflies stay out all night.
  • A softer wind that rises and falls; a stronger wind sound only on windy days.
  • A quieter river.

v0.4 · 27 October 2026

  • Sunbeams through the gaps in the clouds on partly cloudy days, most when the sun is low.

v0.3 · 26 October 2026

  • Butterflies visit the flowers on calm days and sometimes land on them.
  • Leaves drift down from the trees, more when the wind picks up, and float away on the river.
  • Fireflies glow along the river after sunset; dragonflies dart over the water on warm afternoons.
  • Choose how many butterflies, fireflies, dragonflies and falling leaves in Settings → Nature.
  • A quieter wind, and volume stabilization (on by default, in Settings) so storms don't blast.

v0.2 · 25 October 2026

  • The river mirrors the trees, the mountain and the sky, and flows around its rocks.
  • Trees cast leafy shadows that sway with the wind.
  • Sudden gusts, as strong as your local forecast says: you see the trees bend and hear the wind rise.
  • A new wind sound, close thunder louder, and birds visit a little more often.
  • Slower device? Turn reflections or tree shadows off in Settings.

v0.1 · 25 October 2026, the first release

  • A meadow that follows your real day: dawn, sunrise, sunset and night come when they do where you are.
  • Your local weather, live: sun, clouds, rain, storms with lightning, and wind from its real direction.
  • A clock in the sky: digital or analog, six fonts, behind the mountain or in front of everything, in your colours.
  • An alarm that wakes you with a rooster, with snooze.
  • Nature sounds all around you: birds that come and go, crickets and frogs at night, bees, wind, rain, the river, thunder.
  • A night sky with the real moon phase, stars, the Milky Way and a few shooting stars.
  • Drag to look around; zoom with the wheel or a pinch.
  • Choose a place instead of sharing your location. Your settings stay on your device.
